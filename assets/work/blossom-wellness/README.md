# Blossom Wellness case study — evidence

No Blossom screenshots or reports are on the page yet. The "Evidence of the
work" section is ready for them; the HTML comment above that section in
case-studies/blossom-wellness.html has a copy-paste template.

Rules
- Only verified client reports or real work. No recreated screenshots, no
  before/after unless both states are real.
- Keep every number exactly as it appears in the report.
- Remove or blur client names, emails and phone numbers (counseling data is
  sensitive).
- Learning: show build evidence (website, launch assets, campaign setup,
  pre-launch strategy). Do not add weak post-launch performance screenshots.
