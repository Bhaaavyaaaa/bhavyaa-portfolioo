Kyra Clinic — Local digital growth for a plastic & aesthetic surgery clinic

Client
Kyra Aesthetic Clinic
Category
Plastic & Aesthetic Surgery
Role
Digital Marketing Manager
Focus
Ludhiana · Chandigarh · Punjab

Objective

Build stronger local digital visibility for the clinic across search, YouTube and social — with a particular focus on relevant searches in Ludhiana, Chandigarh and Punjab.

Context

Kyra is a plastic and aesthetic surgery clinic. I was the sole point of contact for its digital marketing, responsible for strategy and execution across organic search, content, YouTube and Instagram, alongside Google and Meta advertising.

The main focus was making the clinic easier to find when people were searching for its services and related terms.

Local SEO

Making local search visibility a growth channel.

Ludhiana · Chandigarh · Punjab

I led the clinic’s local SEO strategy across Ludhiana, Chandigarh and Punjab, working across keyword targeting, on-page optimisation, content, website improvements and off-page SEO.

The focus was not simply ranking individual pages. It was building broader search visibility around relevant local and service-related searches.
