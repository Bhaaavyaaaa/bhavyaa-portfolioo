Context

MUVIQ offers complete drive-system solutions for vehicle and industrial platforms — and positions itself as an engineering partner, not a component supplier.

Messaging used in the ads

  • “More Than a Component Supplier. Your Engineering Partner for Complete Drive-System Solutions”
  • “Engineer the Next Generation of Mobility”
  • “Accelerate Time-to-Market with Co-Engineered Drive Systems”
  • “Built for Performance. Engineered for Reliability.”
  • “Great machines demand more than individual components, they require drive system solutions”

Objective

The work was broader than campaign setup: strengthen MUVIQ’s global digital positioning, reach relevant B2B audiences, generate leads and build business connections that support customer acquisition.

  2. Lead generation

  3. Customer acquisition

Conversion / Lead 100%
