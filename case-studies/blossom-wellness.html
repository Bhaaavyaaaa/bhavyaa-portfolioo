Business 01Established businessOnline counseling · California

Blossom WellnessCounseling

From a working business to a more structured digital journey.

Context

Blossom Wellness Counseling is an online counseling business serving clients in California.

When the engagement started, the business was already launched and operating. The challenge was not simply getting more activity — the broader digital journey needed to be structured around a clearer strategy.

My work focused on connecting the different parts of that journey instead of treating the website, campaigns and communication as separate activities.

Counseling — 01 · Strategy

Start withthe journey.

I worked on the overall marketing strategy around the existing business, looking at how users move from discovery to the website, conversion and follow-up.

  1. 01

    Business

    Understand the existing business and its growth needs.

  2. 02

    Audience

    Define the audience and intent behind the acquisition journey.

  3. 03

    Funnel

    Structure the journey from traffic to conversion.

  4. 04

    Channels

    Align search, paid media, website and email around the same objective.

Counseling — 02 · Website + conversion

The website waspart of the funnel.

Website optimisation was handled as part of the acquisition strategy rather than as a standalone design exercise.

Work included

  • Website structure
  • User journey
  • Conversion-focused changes
  • Content optimisation
  • Keyword alignment
  • Page-level improvements
  • Improving the path from traffic to intended action

Counseling — 03 · Search + keywords

Building thesearch foundation.

Keyword research and optimisation were used to align the website with relevant search intent and improve the connection between what people searched for and what the website offered.

  • Keyword research

  • Search intent

  • Content optimisation

  • On-page SEO

Counseling — 04 · Paid acquisition

From strategyto acquisition.

Once the broader journey was structured, paid acquisition became part of the system.

  1. Google Ads

    Search-focused acquisition and campaign planning.

  2. Meta Ads

    Audience-based acquisition and campaign execution.

  3. Conversion

    Connecting campaign traffic back to the website journey and intended action.

Counseling — 05 · Email + referral

The journey didn’t endat the first click.

The work also extended beyond acquisition through email marketing and referral initiatives designed around new and existing client relationships.

  • Retention · follow-up

    Email marketing

    Supporting communication and follow-up with new and existing audiences.

  • Acquisition

    Referral programs

    Supporting new-client acquisition through referral initiatives.

The focus was not one channel.

It was making the different parts of the digital journey work with more intention.
